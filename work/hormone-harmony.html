Case study 03 · Health & wellness · Spec static set

One night, three feelings.

Three static ads for Hormone Harmony by Happy Mammoth, each testing a different emotion around the symptom women in menopause feel every single night: broken sleep.

ProductHormone Harmony (Happy Mammoth)
AngleMenopause sleep & night symptoms
AvatarWomen in menopause losing sleep to hot flashes and 3 AM wake-ups
AwarenessProblem aware
FormatStatic image ads, 4:5
Test3 concepts, same CTA
01 · The opportunity

Lead with the night, not the hormones

Most menopause ads lead with hormones. But that's not what keeps her up. It's the hot flashes, the 3 AM wake-ups, dragging herself through the next day. Sleep is the symptom she feels every single night, so that's where these ads start.

02 · The concepts

Three hooks, three moods

Each static pairs one emotion with its own visual world. Same product proof, same CTA, so the test shows which feeling makes her stop.

Empowerment

"Menopause stole your sleep? Take it back."

Soft pink, product front and centre. Frames sleep as something she can reclaim, not something she has to accept.

Anger

"Hot flashes? 3 AM wakings? F*ck you, menopause."

Dark plum and hot pink. Says out loud what she's thinking at 3 AM, and makes menopause the enemy.

Reflection

"When was the last time menopause let you sleep through the night?"

Night fading into morning pink. A question she can't answer, followed by "you're not alone."

03 · The thinking

Why three emotions

Not every woman in menopause feels the same way about it. Some are ready to take control, some are angry, and some are just tired of being tired.

Instead of guessing which one she is, each static speaks to one of them, and the data decides which feeling sells.

04 · Test plan

How we'd know

Hypothesis: women in menopause respond more to an emotional reframe of their sleepless nights than to product benefits, and one of these three emotions will clearly outperform the others.

What we test

  • 3 concepts: hook + visual mood together
  • Same CTA, offer and landing page across all three

What we watch

  • CTR: which concept stops her and gets the click
  • CPC and CPA vs. the account's current best static

What comes next

  • Winning emotion gets a pure hook test: same visual, new headlines
  • Then test CTAs matched to the winning emotion

Spec concept set built on public information. Not affiliated with Happy Mammoth.
