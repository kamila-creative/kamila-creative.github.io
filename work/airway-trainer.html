Case study 02 · Sleep · Spec iteration brief

One winner, two next moves.

An iteration brief for Airway Trainer – Quit Snoring: breaking down a UGC ad that has been running for eight months, then briefing what to test next, one variable at a time.

ProductAirway Trainer app
Control adUGC talking head
Live sinceMar 16, 2026 (~8 months)
AngleSnoring isn't a nose problem
AwarenessSolution aware
Test3 new hooks + 1 new angle
01 · The control

Why this ad keeps running

Watch the control ad ↗

Brands don't keep paying for ads that lose. This one has run since March, and the brand has about 39 active ads using the same script with different creators. The script is the asset, and the faces rotate.

It opens with the problem itself. She snores on camera before saying a word, so you hear it before you read anything. Then come the real stakes, her husband stopped spending the night with her, and everything she already tried, nasal strips and mouth guards, so anyone who's been there sees herself.

The turn is the mechanism: snoring isn't a nose problem, it's weak muscles in the mouth and throat. That one line makes the app feel new instead of like another snoring fix.

02 · Iteration 1

New hook, same body

HOOK AMy husband recorded me snoring. I didn't believe it was me.
Visual
Phone playing a snoring recording, then a cut to her shocked face.
Captions
MY HUSBAND RECORDED ME SNORING 😳 / I DIDN'T BELIEVE IT WAS ME
HOOK BPOV: he moved to the couch. Again.
Visual
Pillow and blanket on the couch, then a cut to the creator.
Captions
POV: HE MOVED TO THE COUCH. AGAIN.
HOOK CHe started falling asleep on the couch "by accident." Every night.
Visual
Him asleep on the couch with the TV still on, then a cut to the creator raising an eyebrow.
Captions
HE STARTED FALLING ASLEEP ON THE COUCH "BY ACCIDENT" 🙄 / EVERY NIGHT.

Hypothesis: the control's snore hook works because it's instantly recognizable. A hook that shows the consequence for the relationship will beat it on hook rate.

03 · Iteration 2

New angle, same format

Same talking-head UGC style. What changes is the persona and the stakes.

AvatarWomen 25–35 in a new relationship, embarrassed rather than in a long marriage
HOOKI was terrified to stay over at my new boyfriend's place.

Because I snored like this…

Yeah. Not exactly first-sleepover energy.

So I had a system. I'd stay awake until he fell asleep first, or suddenly remember I had an early meeting.

I tried nasal strips, mouth guards, I even slept sitting up once. Nothing was working.

Then I found out the cause of snoring isn't your nose at all. It's weak muscles in your mouth and throat that relax too much when you sleep.

And I thought, wait, if I strengthen them, will it actually stop?

Turns out there are dozens and dozens of studies on it.

So I downloaded this app, and I literally do the exercises while I'm watching TV.

By week two I was waking up way more refreshed. By week five, my snoring was completely gone.

Last weekend I fell asleep first. On purpose.

Honestly, everybody should be strengthening their airway.

Notes for the creator

  • Natural lighting and a casual setting, like a couch or a car.
  • A light, self-deprecating tone, where she's laughing at herself instead of sounding ashamed.
  • Captions on throughout the whole video, so it still works with the sound off while people scroll.

Hypothesis: the control only speaks to people whose snoring is already hurting a long relationship. Fear before it becomes a problem reaches a younger, bigger audience the brand isn't targeting yet.

04 · Test plan

How we'd know

Hooks first, because they're the cheapest to make and have the biggest impact. Then the new angle, which opens a new audience.

What we test

  • Round 1: hooks A–C vs. the control
  • Round 2: the new-relationship angle vs. the control

What we watch

  • Hook rate (3-second views) and hold to 0:15
  • Cost per install and install-to-trial rate, since it's an app

What comes next

  • A hook wins: re-cut it onto the other creator videos the brand already runs
  • The new angle wins: brief 3 more creators in that persona
